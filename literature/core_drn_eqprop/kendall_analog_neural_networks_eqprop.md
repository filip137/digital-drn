## Summary

This is the key analog nonlinear resistive network and EqProp reference. It
shows how programmable conductances and nonlinear circuit elements can form an
energy-based analog network trained with local voltage-drop updates. It is the
main reminder that DRNs are implicit physical systems, not just ordinary MLPs.

## Use For

- Nonlinear resistive networks.
- Conductance updates.
- Analog EqProp motivation.
- Local learning and physical implementation.
